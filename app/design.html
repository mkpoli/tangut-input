Sources

  • Alexander Zorin, “On a ‘Golden’ Khara-Khoto Manuscript Preserved in the Institute of Oriental Manuscripts, RAS”, Revue d’Études Tibétaines 56 (2020), 161–169. Manuscript XT-180: indigo paper, gold ink.

Rules

  • One ring: 2px of the accent, 2px outside the control. A component may inset it when an edge would clip it, never recolour it.
  • State is never colour alone: a check, a thumb, a rule, a word or an outline carries it too.
  • Three surfaces only: the page, a panel, an inset. Overlays are the one place that casts a shadow.
  • Text clears 4.5:1 on the surface it sits on; a boundary that identifies a control clears 3:1.
  • Native elements for controls. A custom widget has to bring its own keyboard, and the browser already has one.
  • Controls and their labels meet the 24px target; a chip is 26px and a row is at least 40px.
  • Reduced motion collapses transitions; forced colours keep every shape.
