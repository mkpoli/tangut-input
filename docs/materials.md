# Materials

Every source this site draws on, every one found while looking for more, and the ones that do not
exist anywhere yet. A source is listed with what it actually gives and the terms it comes under; a
source with no licence stated is not free to redistribute at scale, so the site links to it where it
cannot copy it.

## Books, keyed

The editions the values CCAMC carried are keyed from, each row citing the edition and page. The
scans are large and sit out of the build in `cache/books/`; `data/books/source.yaml` gives each work,
what it supplies and its terms, and `scripts/fetch_books.py` fetches them again and checks the md5.

| Work | Gives | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 番漢合時掌中珠, 骨勒茂才, 1190 | the Chinese character written for a Tangut character, and the Chinese gloss | public domain; the keying photographs (清光山西巖寺 copy, 貽安堂 printing, [DOI 10.20730/100354804](https://kokusho.nijl.ac.jp/biblio/100354804)) are CC BY-NC 4.0 |
| 夏漢字典, 李范文, 中國社會科學出版社, 1997 | Li Fanwen's reading with its fanqie and 直音, and the Chinese meanings | none stated |
| 文海研究, 史金波・白濱・黃振華, 中國社會科學出版社, 1983 | the 文海's entries and its fanqie | none stated |
| Тангутская филология, Н. А. Невский, Издательство восточной литературы, 1960 | the 音（聂历山）column: Nevsky's readings | none stated by the Institute of Oriental Manuscripts, which scanned it |
| 西夏語通韻字典, 荒川慎太郎, 1999 | Arakawa's reconstruction, syllable by syllable | none stated |
| 西夏語文研究論文集, 龔煌城, 中研院語言學研究所籌備處, 2002 | Gong Hwang-cherng's reconstruction, and his study of the transcriptions in 掌中珠 | none stated |

The Chinese 直音 follows 掌中珠 first, since it prints a character for a character; the fanqie the
rhyme books spell the same character with are the second warrant, and 夏漢字典 and the other
dictionaries print both beside their readings.

## Linked, not copied

- [CCAMC](http://ccamc.org/) — per-character pages with 音（李范文）and its fanqie and 直音, 音（聂历山）, a 藏文對音 field, 聲韻, 同音, 擬音 for eight systems, and the 文海 commentary. HTTP only: the host serves no TLS. It blocks bursts, and it states no licence. Since 2026-11-04 the site copies none of its readings or glosses: a card links to `http://ccamc.org/tangut.php?n4694=<char>`, and the fields it carried are rebuilt from the editions above. Until they are, the two systems the site does not display (Gong Hwang-cherng and Arakawa Shintarō) and CCAMC's English and Chinese glosses survive only as normalized terms in `app/data/tangut-ccamc-index.json.gz`, which answers a search and is never rendered. The 文海 text is public domain and comes from `data/wenhai/entry-text.json.gz`. The site does not crawl CCAMC.

## Available, not used

- [TangutSeg](https://github.com/jiangli-va/TangutSeg) — word segmentation over 2,750 annotated sentences, a 19,000-entry lexicon and 320,000 characters of unlabelled text. Its README states the full data will not be published; the repository carries code and three example files only.
- 西田龍雄, 西夏語韻図『五音切韻』の研究, 京都大學文學部研究紀要, in three parts: [（上）](http://hdl.handle.net/2433/73009) 20 (1981): 91–147; [（中）](http://hdl.handle.net/2433/73012) 21 (1982): 1–100; [（下）](http://hdl.handle.net/2433/73016) 22 (1983): 1–187. Scanned PDFs with a rough OCR layer. （中） and （下） reproduce and analyse the 五音切韻's rhyme charts (「衆漂海入門」) from level 1 / rising 1 to level 94 / rising 86, pieced together from several Kozlov-collection copies; the charts for level 95–97 do not survive and are rebuilt from the 文海. A level rhyme and the rising rhyme paired with it share one chart, and an unpaired rhyme (level 6, rising 20) has its own; the pairs drift apart in number, level 33 going with rising 30 and level 93 with rising 86, and level 94 standing alone, though Nishida holds that its rising forms existed (（下）, p. 171). The Chart page also sets both tones of a rhyme side by side, grouped by Han Xiaomang's 総合韻. Of the charts whose headings could be read, about 80 of 106, every pairing agrees with the 総合韻 except that four characters of rising 60 share level 68's class, where Nishida's chart 71 has level 68 alone and pairs rising 60 with level 67 (（下）, p. 110). Chart 74's heading and first sentence print 平声74韻 where its figure caption, the rest of its text and the sequence give level 71 (（下）, pp. 119–120). Each chart spans two facing pages, nine rows by five columns, with 重唇・輕唇, 舌頭・舌上 and 齒頭・正齒 each sharing a column. Rows 1–4 (rarely 5) are types of final, 開口 and 合口 in two series, and hold only syllables with a voiceless unaspirated (全清) initial, a circle marking an empty cell; rows 5–6 use only the middle column, for 流風音 (l-, ž-, ńź-), two to five to a cell; rows 7 and 8 hold the level and rising rhymes' representative characters, which name the chart (（中）, pp. 1–2; （下）, p. 1). It charts syllable types, not every character, so it cannot feed the page without transcribing the charts.
- [BabelStone 文海](https://www.babelstone.co.uk/Tangut/WenhaiLookup.js) and [同音](https://www.babelstone.co.uk/Tangut/TongyinLookup.js) lookups, Andrew West — JavaScript arrays mapping Li Fanwen 2008 numbers to code points and to page and line in the 文海 and both editions of the 同音. Positions only, without fanqie or class; a cross-check for Tangut-rhyme-dictionaries-data. The 文海 lookup also shows a photograph of each woodblock page (`Wenhai/Wenhai_L05A.jpg` for level-tone folio 5, first page), about 650 × 980 pixels. No licence stated.
- [tinbreaker/tangutdata](https://github.com/tinbreaker/tangutdata) — one JSON of 6,090 characters with 同音 positions, 文海 rhyme, tone, 小韻 and fanqie, and several reconstructions. Its columns follow Tangut-rhyme-dictionaries-data, so it looks like a merged copy of sources already in use. MIT for the repository; its sources keep their own terms.
- [番漢合時掌中珠, 1924 lithograph](https://www.shuge.org/view/fan_han_he_shi_zhang_zhong_zhu/) — page images on 書格. Answered HTTP 525 on 2026-11-03, so the scan itself is unchecked.
- [Digital Silk Road, 東洋文庫](https://dsr.nii.ac.jp/toyobunko/) — manuscript images, for attestation rather than data.
- [Tibetan three dialects G2P](https://khepa-nyima.github.io/tibetan-three-dialects-g2p/) — not Tangut, but the nearest thing to a system for reading a Tibetan transcription aloud if one is ever collected.

## Missing

- **番漢合時掌中珠 as data.** Keying has started in the site's `zhangzhongzhu` tables: the columns, their order and the section headings have to be settled band by band from the canvas images before a count exists. A reading of a damaged glyph waits for a second witness. Nothing on the page shows the tables yet.
- **藏文對音 beyond five manuscripts.** Tibetan transcriptions of Tangut survive in fragments and are discussed one article at a time. Only the five British Library fragments in BabelStone's index are tabulated.
- **Tangut seal and cursive forms.** Seal script is confined to the headings of a few steles and cursive to manuscripts; no face and no per-character table exists, so the site shows the regular script only.
